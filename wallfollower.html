Wall Follower Algorithm

The Wall Follower algorithm (also known as the right-hand rule or left-hand rule) is one of the simplest maze-solving techniques. By keeping one hand continuously touching the wall on one side and walking forward, you are guaranteed to reach the exit in any simply connected maze where the entrance and exit are connected to the outer perimeter.

Every step in Wall Follower goes:

1. Place hand on the chosen wall (e.g. right side)
2. Check open directions relative to current facing orientation
3a. If the right side is open, turn right and take a step.
3b. If the right side is blocked but straight ahead is open, step forward.
3c. If straight is also blocked, turn left until an open path is found.
3d. If it's the finish, complete.
